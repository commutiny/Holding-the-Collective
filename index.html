Self-assessment · Eleven capacities, four depths

Holding the Collective

Who this is for: people doing collectivising work — holding space where a lot of different people, interests and views need to move together.

For each capacity, work through the levels in order, shallowest first. Answer Yes only if you usually, mostly, or always show that behaviour — not just once. The moment you can't honestly say Yes, stop there: that's your depth for now, not a mark against you. Add a line on why for each level you answer.

Capacity language draws on Vyaktitva's framework of adaptive, inside-out leadership.

Swimming Pool
Shallow end. You can do this in a safe, structured setting — familiar people, low stakes, a script to follow.
Lake / Lagoon
Calmer open water. You carry it into everyday situations, though conditions are still fairly forgiving.
Open Ocean
You hold it steady in real, changing, high-stakes situations — no script, and it still works.
Challenger Deep
Even under maximum pressure, when it would be easiest to protect yourself — this is where you go on instinct.
